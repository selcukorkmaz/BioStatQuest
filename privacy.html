● Privacy How your data is handled

Plain-English privacy.

No ads. No tracking cookies. No data sales. Here's what that actually means, section by section.

Last updated: April 2026

The short version

Your progress is yours

Play as a guest and your data never leaves your browser. Sign in and the only things we keep are your email and your progress JSON — encrypted in transit, never shared, deletable on request. No behavioral profiling, no advertising, no dark patterns.

If you want the detail, every category below is written to be checkable rather than reassuring.

What we collect, and don't

Category by category

Guest mode

If you play without signing in, your progress — XP, streaks, answered questions, spaced-repetition schedule — is stored only in your browser via localStorage. Nothing is sent to our servers. Clear your browser data and your progress is gone with it — sign in to preserve it across devices.

Accounts (when you sign in)

Authentication runs on Supabase. If you sign in we keep exactly two things:

  • Your email address, for login.
  • Your progress JSON — XP, streaks, SRS schedule, per-case scores.

We do not share or sell this data. Ever. Delete your account and everything attached to it any time by emailing info@biostatquest.com.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, cookie-less analytics (Plausible). It reports aggregate page views and anonymized device type. No personal identifiers. No cross-site tracking. No advertising.

Cookies

Only essential session cookies when you're signed in — nothing else. No advertising cookies, no tracking cookies, no consent banners (because there's nothing to consent to).

Children

BioStat Quest is intended for adult learners (16+). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We'll update this page if anything material changes and note the date above. Substantive updates will also surface a one-time notice on next sign-in.

Questions, or want your data deleted?

Email info@biostatquest.com. Real person, real reply, usually within a few days.
