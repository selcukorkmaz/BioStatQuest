What institutions get

Features built for teaching a cohort

Assign cases as structured homework

Pick specific cases or entire branches that map to your syllabus. Students work through them on their own schedule; you track completion, accuracy, and time-per-case at the class level. No more "read chapter 7 and we'll discuss it Thursday" with no way to know who actually did.

Method deep-dives where students stumble

Every wrong answer routes the student to that method's deep-dive panel — intuition, formula, assumptions, common pitfalls, and cited sources. A student who misses a confidence-interval question sees the CI explainer. One who misses a Cox-model question sees survival. The remediation is automatic and specific; you don't have to write it.

Class-level cohort dashboard

Your instructor view shows aggregate accuracy per branch (foundations, probability, regression, study design, causal, Bayesian, etc.), flagged weak areas across the class, and per-student status. You teach to the actual gap — not the gap you assumed from last year's cohort.

Syllabus-aligned collections

We curate case bundles to match your course — MPH methods, USMLE Step 1 biostats, residency didactics, doctoral quals. With 50 cases and 1,000+ questions spanning foundations through causal inference and Bayesian methods, there's plenty to work with. You keep what maps, we drop what doesn't.

Bulk seats, one invoice

A single institutional subscription covers your whole cohort. Students get full Pro access for the duration of your licence. Renews annually; we invoice through standard academic procurement (net-30 or net-45). No per-student sign-up friction for your course coordinator to manage.

Frequently asked

Before you email us

Is this FERPA-compliant? ▾

We collect email and progress data only; no identifiable grades or PII beyond the learner's own account. For institutions with stricter requirements we can execute a data-processing addendum specifying retention, deletion, and incident-notification terms. Ask about our standard DPA template.

Can students use their institutional email? ▾

Yes. Single sign-on via Google OAuth works with most institutional Google Workspace domains (*.edu, *.ac.uk, *.edu.tr, etc.). Email-with-OTP works with any institutional inbox. No student credential harvesting; we never see a password.

Do students keep access after the course? ▾

Institutional seats are tied to your subscription period. When the licence ends, students can convert to an individual Pro subscription at the standard personal rate and keep all their progress, streak, and spaced-repetition history. No data is lost or reset.

Can we preview before committing? ▾

Yes — a free 2-week pilot for you and 2–3 colleagues to evaluate the workflow and content fit. You see the instructor dashboard with your own accounts acting as the cohort. Email us to schedule.

How long does setup take? ▾

Typically 3–5 business days from first email to student-ready, including the syllabus-alignment call. Multi-program institutional deals take 1–2 weeks to align procurement. We can work to an academic-year start date if you email at least two weeks ahead.

Can we bring our own questions? ▾

Yes, with some scoping. Custom case authoring is included for program-wide and institutional plans (typically 2–10 bespoke cases aligned to your specific syllabus). Smaller plans use the shared library only.
