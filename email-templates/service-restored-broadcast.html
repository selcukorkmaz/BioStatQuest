What happened

A configuration problem on our end caused our database to be suspended by our hosting provider. Everything behind your account — signing in, saving progress, XP, streaks — stopped working.

The public pages of the site stayed up the whole time, which made the outage easy to miss from the outside. That includes us, and that's the part we're least happy about.

Your progress is intact

Nothing was lost. The database was suspended, not deleted — your account, XP, streaks, completed cases and badges are exactly as you left them. Sign in and you'll pick up from the same place.

So it doesn't happen again

We've added automated checks that test the actual sign-in path around the clock. If authentication ever breaks again, we'll know within minutes instead of days.
